06 / WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

A workbench, not an explanation shortcut.

Architecture: token embeddings + sinusoidal positions → two causal, two-head attention blocks with residuals → output logits. No MLP, normalization, dropout, autograd, external model or API. The browser loads complete Python-trained weights, not precomputed intermediate traces. It does not train or reproduce Python's random generator.

Training: dim 8, heads 2, layers 2, 100 epochs × 64 fresh examples, seed 7, SGD 0.1, global clipping 1.0. Training loss is online mean loss, not a held-out measurement. Float64 arithmetic is compared within tolerance, not cross-runtime bitwise identity. Effects describe one seed and task. A one-layer gap or classic induction mechanism is not established by this workbench.

The stable log-sum-exp loss also fixes a numerical inconsistency: the old loss clipped very unlikely targets at about 27.63 nats while its gradient remained nonzero. Extreme-logit finite differences now agree with the reported loss.
